x2-perception

Perceptive locomotion for the AgiBot X2 humanoid. ACP, originally developed on the Unitree G1 with a LiDAR, re-implemented for the X2 and driven by a custom chest-mounted depth camera.

Approach

The X2's built-in depth camera cannot see the ground close to its feet, where every foothold is placed. We add a depth camera on a 3D-printed chest mount and train a depth student that turns its image, with the robot's own proprioception, into the height maps ACP needs. ACP is re-implemented for the X2 and trained from scratch in simulation; everything is scored by a held-out MuJoCo evaluation on a replica of the IEEE Humanoids 2026 loco-manipulation apparatus that never appears in training.

Real robot

The same policy on the real X2, zero-shot (no training on the robot), running on its onboard computer with the chest depth camera: it stands steadily, recovers from pushes, and walks an outdoor course of patio, gravel, a step down and a doorway under gamepad control.
